# Archetype — Turnaround

**Skepticism weight: 4 (high).**

## Definition
A business that has been performing badly is being fixed. Returns come from the gap
between current depressed economics and a normalised level the company has either
demonstrated before or that comparable assets earn today.

## Return source
Recovery in margins/returns on a roughly stable revenue base, plus whatever multiple the
market awards once the recovery is believed. **Turnarounds very often carry a re-rating
leg — decompose it and check `re-rating.md` separately if it exceeds 40%.**

## `must_be_true`
1. **The cause of underperformance is diagnosed, specifically.** Not "weak execution".
   Which assets, which contracts, which cost line, which management decision.
   *Establishes with:* segment-level or asset-level economics showing where the loss sits.
2. **The fix is structural, not cyclical.** Distinguish "we are doing something
   different" from "conditions improved". If the recovery would have happened anyway, this
   is `cyclical-recovery.md`, which has a different checklist.
3. **The fix has already started and is visible in a reported number.** A plan is not
   evidence. At least one quarter of in-period improvement in the specific metric that
   was broken.
4. **The normalised level is anchored.** Either the company's own pre-deterioration
   economics, or what comparable assets earn now. State which, and why it is reachable.
5. **The balance sheet survives the journey.** Turnarounds fail on funding before they
   fail on strategy. Net debt/EBITDA path, refinancing calendar, covenant headroom.

## Standard evidence pattern
Asset-by-asset or segment-by-segment P&L showing where value leaks → the specific
intervention (renovation, rebranding, closure, renegotiation, new management) → early
in-period evidence → the normalised economics of a comparable asset → the funding bridge.

## Standard failure mode
**The turnaround is priced as complete before it is complete.** The forecast jumps to
normalised economics in year two with no partial-progress path, and the target multiple
is set at the peer level as though the recovery were finished and de-risked. Second
failure: **serial extension** — each year the recovery is a year away.

## Falsifiers
- Two consecutive quarters with no improvement in the specific broken metric.
- The fix requires capital the company cannot fund without dilution.
- Management changes the definition of the target metric.
- The normalised benchmark itself deteriorates (peers de-rate operationally).

## Sectors where it recurs
Hotels, airlines, PSU banks, capital goods after an order-book collapse, telecom,
distressed real estate, any asset-heavy business bought out of insolvency.

## Corpus examples
- **SAMHI Hotels** (Yes Securities, Sep-2025) — the corpus's clearest turnaround. Strong
  on conditions 1, 2 and 4: the fix is named (acquire below replacement cost, renovate,
  re-brand), the discipline is quantified (discount to replacement cost), and the
  capability is proven by a realised disposal at ~20x EV/EBITDA. Condition 5 is addressed
  through a dated gross-debt reduction plan of INR 3,000mn over FY26-30E. Its weakness is
  the re-rating leg bolted on top (see `re-rating.md`).
