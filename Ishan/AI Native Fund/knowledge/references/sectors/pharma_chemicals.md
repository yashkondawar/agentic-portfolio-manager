# Pharma & Chemicals — Sector Playbook (generics, APIs, CDMO/CRAMS, specialty chem, agrochem)

Synthesized superset of `registry/kpis/pharma_chemicals.yaml` and the
external ER project's pharma sector pack. KPI vocabulary:
`registry/kpis/pharma_chemicals.yaml`; deep KPI definitions:
`knowledge/data/kpis/micro/pharma_chemicals.yaml`. Sector cycle index:
NIFTY PHARMA — see `config/settings.yaml -> sector_index_map`.

## Core truth

Commodity generics erode; value accrues to complexity (injectables,
biosimilars, validated specialty molecules) and to compliance track
record. NPPA/NLEM caps the domestic price umbrella; USFDA status caps the
US one.

## Qualitative lenses

- **Complexity moat**: manufacturing-process complexity; mix shift toward
  complex generics/injectables/biosimilars; CDMO/CRAMS depth — client
  validation cycles are switching costs.
- **Backward integration**: KSM/API self-sufficiency %, PLI benefit
  capture, China-dependence of inputs.
- **Compliance**: USFDA/EDQM/DCGI history per facility (warning letters,
  import alerts, OAI/VAI), quantified revenue at risk per facility,
  remediation credibility and management commentary.
- **Pipeline**: ANDA stock/flow, Paragraph-IV filings and success rate,
  first-to-file value; for specialty chem — molecules under multi-year
  contracts, clients' patent-cliff calendar.
- **China+1**: evidenced share shifts in CDMO/API sourcing (not just
  narrative).
- **Domestic**: NLEM exposure % of revenue, chronic vs acute mix, MR
  productivity; key chronic therapies dominating domestic sales and their
  growth stability.
- **US generics**: price-erosion rate trend from peer commentary.
- **Specialty chemicals**: active-principles focus and the nature of
  multi-year client validation moats (high switching costs).

## Cycle overlay

- **Valuation vs earnings cycle**: is the premium justified by
  sustainable ROCE stability, or driven purely by the valuation cycle
  (Stage 3 euphoria)? Run the eight-phase read on NIFTY PHARMA P/E
  percentiles (`index_data`, 2016-onward).
- **Credit/capex cycle**: capex/growth funding needs vs the current
  credit window; resulting FCF constraints and debt risk.
- **Policy/profit cycle**: PLI/regulatory moat sustainability; NPPA price
  caps as the domestic policy-cycle risk channel.

## Niche pointers

- **Specialty chemicals**: revenue stability and margin profile of
  CDMO/CRAMS segments vs core generics/branded business.
- **Paragraph IV track record**: peer group's Para-IV filing success rate
  over the last 3-5 years.

## Relative-valuation justifier

Premium vs compliance cleanliness + high-margin mix (CDMO/specialty
share) + pipeline optionality — a clean-facility CDMO deserves different
math than a commodity-generic exporter; never blend them silently.

## Preferred sources

USFDA inspection classification database, company facility disclosures,
NPPA/NLEM lists, PLI notifications, IQVIA citations for domestic market
shares.
