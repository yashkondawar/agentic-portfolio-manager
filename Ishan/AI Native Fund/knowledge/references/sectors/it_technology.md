# IT & Technology — Sector Playbook (services, ER&D, SaaS, new-age/platforms)

Synthesized superset of `registry/kpis/it_technology.yaml` and the
external ER project's IT sector pack. KPI vocabulary:
`registry/kpis/it_technology.yaml`; deep KPI definitions:
`knowledge/data/kpis/micro/it_technology.yaml`. Sector cycle index:
NIFTY IT — see `config/settings.yaml -> sector_index_map`.

## Core truth

Human-capital arbitrage -> asset-light high ROCE; the durability question
is non-linearity (revenue per employee) and AI's effect on both
utilization and pricing.

## Qualitative lenses

- **Non-linear growth**: revenue growth vs headcount growth divergence;
  platform/IP revenue share; niche digital talent pool; sustained pricing
  power.
- **Margin levers**: pyramid structure (fresher %), subcontracting %,
  offshore/onsite mix, utilization — who has slack left.
- **Demand**: client-sector IT budgets (US/EU BFSI, retail, hi-tech),
  large-deal TCV pipeline and tenure, vendor-consolidation win/loss.
- **AI/GenAI disruption (mandatory)**: effect on pricing of core
  services, productivity pass-through demands from clients, each peer's
  monetization posture — evidence, not vendor slogans. Impact on
  utilization rates and pricing power for core services.
- **Talent**: attrition trend (replacement-cost drag), wage inflation for
  niche skills (cloud/cyber/data).
- **FX**: extent of the USD-revenue / INR-cost natural hedge; hedging
  policy.
- **Business model**: how the asset-light, human-capital-arbitrage model
  translates into observed high ROCE — verify the mechanism, don't assume
  it.

## Cycle overlay

- **Valuation vs earnings cycle**: is the P/E premium from genuine ROCE
  improvement or valuation-cycle expansion (Stage 3 euphoria)? Run the
  eight-phase read on NIFTY IT P/E percentiles (`index_data`,
  2016-onward).
- **Credit/capex cycle**: assess capex/growth funding needs against the
  current credit window; quantify resulting FCF constraints and debt risk
  (mostly relevant for new-age names, not the cash-rich majors).
- **Policy/profit cycle**: regulatory-moat exposure (PLI etc.) and
  government-counterparty receivable-day risk where applicable.

## Niche pointers

- **ER&D**: is revenue stickier than generic IT services? % of revenue
  from ER&D.
- **SaaS**: NRR strongly positive? gross churn, ARR growth, Rule of 40.
- **New-age tech**: take rate, detailed path to EBITDA breakeven, CAC/LTV
  trends.

## Relative-valuation justifier

P/E premium vs revenue-per-employee trajectory, attrition differential,
and evidenced AI-defense — growth quality over growth quantity.

## Preferred sources

Company factsheets (they disclose utilization/attrition), NASSCOM,
Gartner/IDC citations, USD reporting where available.
