# Consumer & Retail — Sector Playbook (FMCG, discretionary, durables, QSR, liquor)

Synthesized superset of `registry/kpis/consumer_retail.yaml` and the
external ER project's consumer sector pack. KPI vocabulary:
`registry/kpis/consumer_retail.yaml`; deep KPI definitions:
`knowledge/data/kpis/micro/consumer_retail.yaml`. Sector cycle index:
NIFTY FMCG — see `config/settings.yaml -> sector_index_map`.

## Core truth

The moat is distribution + brand; the P&L tell is gross-margin stability
through input cycles (pricing power) and the split of growth between
volume and premiumization.

## Qualitative lenses

- **Pricing power**: GM expansion/hold through commodity cycles;
  price-hike absorption evidence; consumer elasticity by category.
- **Distribution moat**: rural reach, direct-coverage outlets, last-mile
  efficiency; Q-commerce/DTC success and cannibalization risk vs GT/MT;
  sustainability of the moat as channels shift.
- **Volume vs premiumization**: which is driving value growth per peer;
  new-category success rates.
- **Monsoon & rural**: correlate FMCG peer sales with Southwest Monsoon
  data, MSP/farm-income, and consumption sentiment where the mix is
  rural-heavy.
- **Rivalry read (Porter's)**: A&P intensity trend, discounting
  behaviour, private-label/D2C insurgent pressure; bargaining power of
  suppliers (agri-commodities, crude derivatives) on COGS.
- **Brand strength / DTC**: DTC success and cannibalization risk between
  online and traditional retail channels.

## Cycle overlay

- **Valuation vs earnings cycle**: is the premium justified by GM
  stability and distribution-driven ROCE stability, or valuation-cycle
  expansion? Consumer staples often sit in their own Phase 1-2 precisely
  when the broad market is in Phase 4-5 (defensive crowding) — check the
  sector-relative percentile, not just the absolute. Run the eight-phase
  read on NIFTY FMCG P/E percentiles (`index_data`, 2016-onward).
- **Credit/capex cycle**: mostly low capex-intensity; watch retail
  expansion capex (store rollout) vs the credit window for
  QSR/retail names.
- **Policy/profit cycle**: state excise regimes (liquor), GST changes,
  and rural-transfer policy as demand-cycle drivers.

## Niche pointers

- **QSR**: SSSG/LFL trend and average store payback period.
- **Consumer durables / discretionary**: housing/retail linkage, channel
  inventory; correlate with US/EU housing where export-exposed.
- **Liquor / beverages**: state-wise pricing and taxation impact on
  margins and route-to-market changes.

## Relative-valuation justifier

Premium/discount vs GM stability (pricing power) and distribution-driven
ROCE stability.

## Preferred sources

Company decks/transcripts, NielsenIQ/Kantar citations in press, IMD
monsoon data, state excise policies (liquor), Screener/Tickertape for
peer financials.
