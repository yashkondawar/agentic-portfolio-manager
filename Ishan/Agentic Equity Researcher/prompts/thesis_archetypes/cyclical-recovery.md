# Archetype — Cyclical recovery

**Skepticism weight: 4 (medium-high).**

## Definition
The company is a price-taker or volume-taker in a cyclical industry, the cycle is at or
near a trough, and normalisation drives earnings up sharply from a depressed base.

## Return source
Earnings recovery through operating leverage. **The multiple should normally
*compress* as earnings recover** — cyclicals deserve high multiples on trough earnings
and low multiples on peak earnings. A cyclical thesis that also assumes multiple
expansion is usually double-counting.

## `must_be_true`
1. **Cycle position is evidenced, not asserted.** Where are we against a 7–15 year
   history of the relevant spread/price/utilisation? Publish the series.
2. **The trough mechanism is identified.** Demand destruction, oversupply, input spike,
   inventory destocking, policy — each normalises on a different clock.
3. **The supply side is quantified.** For commodities this is the supply-demand balance:
   existing capacity + announced additions − curtailments vs demand. Without it, "prices
   should improve" is a hope.
4. **The company survives and participates.** Cost-curve position (which quartile), and
   balance-sheet capacity to reach the recovery without dilution.
5. **The earnings base used for valuation is mid-cycle, not trough or peak.**

## Standard evidence pattern
Long price/spread history with cycle turning points marked → supply-demand balance table
→ cost-curve position → operating leverage (incremental margin on the next unit) →
mid-cycle earnings → multiple applied to mid-cycle, explicitly stated as such.

## Standard failure mode
**Peak-on-peak, or trough-on-trough.** Applying a peak multiple to peak earnings (double
optimism) or refusing to buy at a trough because the trailing multiple looks high (the
mirror error). Second failure: **calling the trough early** — cycles overshoot, and a
balance sheet that cannot survive two more bad years makes the timing call existential.

## Falsifiers
- Announced supply additions exceed the demand recovery in the balance table.
- The spread makes a new low after the "trough" call.
- Inventory in the channel rises rather than falls.
- The company funds the wait with debt, moving the thesis toward `balance-sheet-repair`.

## Sectors where it recurs
Metals, cement, chemicals, sugar, shipping, paper, commercial vehicles, capital goods,
real estate.
