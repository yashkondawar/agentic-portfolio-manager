# Archetype — Cyclical peak (bear)

**Skepticism weight: 4 (medium-high) — applied symmetrically.**

## Definition
The company is earning at or near a cyclical high. Current earnings are not the run-rate,
and the market is capitalising them as though they were.

## Return source
Negative: earnings mean-reversion, usually amplified by multiple compression as the
market recognises the peak. One of the two bear archetypes.

## Why it needs a bar too
The corpus contains almost no negative initiations (one REDUCE in 34), so there is no
professional template to copy here. That makes it *more* important that our bear cases
are held to the same evidential standard as our bulls — an unfalsifiable bear thesis is
as defective as an unfalsifiable bull one, and cheaper to write.

## `must_be_true`
1. **Current margin/spread/realisation sits in the top quartile of its own 7–15 year
   history.** Publish the series; do not assert "elevated".
2. **The cause of the peak is identified and is cyclical** — a supply outage, a demand
   spike, an input collapse, a policy window, a one-off inventory gain.
3. **The reversion mechanism is named and dated** — announced capacity coming on stream,
   demand normalising, the input recovering, the duty lapsing. Include the supply-demand
   balance.
4. **Normalised earnings are computed**, not just described, and the current multiple is
   restated against them. "Cheap on trailing, expensive on normalised" is the whole
   argument and it must be shown arithmetically.
5. **The company has no structural offset** — no commissioned integration, permanent cost
   advance or mix shift that legitimately raises its mid-cycle economics above history.
   Condition 5 is what separates a genuine peak call from refusing to accept improvement.

## Standard failure mode
**Calling the peak too early and staying wrong through the whole upcycle**, because
"above history" was mistaken for "unsustainable". Structural improvement genuinely
happens: integration commissions, high-cost capacity closes permanently, industries
consolidate. Condition 5 exists to force that possibility into the analysis.

## Falsifiers
- A structural change is identified that legitimately raises mid-cycle economics.
- Announced supply is cancelled or delayed beyond the forecast period.
- The company hedges or contracts forward, locking in the peak (verify tenor and coverage).
- Demand growth absorbs the incoming supply in the balance table.

## Sectors where it recurs
Metals, cement, chemicals, sugar, shipping, commodity chemicals, and any business whose
margin is a spread.
