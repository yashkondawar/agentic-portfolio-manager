## PART 4 — Portfolio Risk Analysis (ranked highest → lowest)

1. **Single biggest risk — Factor concentration / correlated beta.** ~47% sits in two policy-and-sentiment-driven themes (capital-market infra + defence) plus a third (rail/infra). In a risk-off market these de-rate *together*. This dwarfs every single-stock risk.
2. **Valuation risk.** BSE (PE~66), MCX (~54), CDSL (~60), MAZDOCK/GRSE (P/B 10–12×), RVNL (~58 on falling EPS). The biggest winners are also the most expensive — drawdowns can be 30–50%.
3. **Sector-concentration risk.** Defence (~22%) and exchanges (~25%) — single regulatory/budget shocks (SEBI derivatives curbs; MoD budget pause) hit clusters wholesale.
4. **Earnings risk.** Exchange volumes (derivatives-curb-sensitive), defence execution lumpiness, RVNL/rail margin compression, chemicals down-cycle.
5. **Regulatory risk.** SEBI (BSE/MCX/CDSL/CAMS derivatives & fees), CERC market-coupling (IEX), IRCTC convenience fee, US tariffs (chemicals/WAAREE).
6. **Balance-sheet risk.** Concentrated in IRB (leverage), PSU banks (IOB), POLYPLEX/PRINCEPIPE/LXCHEM (commodity WC swings). Core book is net-cash, so this is *contained*.
7. **Management risk.** Lowest — mostly competent PSU/private managers; isolated flags (CLEAN promoter sale, IOB, weak EPC names).
